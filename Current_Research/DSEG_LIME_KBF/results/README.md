# Result files

These are compact outputs from the completed pilot and fold-0 sentinel. Patient identifiers are dataset identifiers; no names or direct identifiers are included.

- `pilot_manifest.csv`: balanced 20-case selection.
- `faithfulness_cases.csv`: deletion faithfulness by case.
- `complete_evaluation_summary.json`: random controls and progressive-curve summary.
- `stability_summary.csv`: three-seed stability on six cases.
- `sample_sensitivity_summary.csv`: 64/128/256 perturbation comparison.
- `fold0_metrics.csv`: held-out metrics for original and signed variants.
- `PILOT_REPORT_FR.md`: complete French interpretation of the pilot.

These exploratory results must not be presented as a completed five-fold clinical validation.
